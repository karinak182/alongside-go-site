How it works

From a spreadsheet to a planned week in a few minutes.

1

Add the people you see

Upload your spreadsheet or type them in. For each one, say where, how long, and which days and times work.

2

Plan the week

One tap places everyone around your lunch, breaks and driving limits. Drag any visit to change it, and undo if you change your mind.

3

Text and go

Messages open on your own phone, already filled in. Send your day to Google or Apple Maps and head out.

Privacy

Your client list stays with you.

  • Client details are saved on your own device and locked with a passcode only you know.
  • We have no database of your clients. We can't see your list, and we can't reset your passcode.
  • Use it on your phone and computer with private sync. What is synced is scrambled before it leaves your device.
  • Only initials are needed. You choose what to enter.

One thing to know. To find a street address on the map and get driving times, the app sends the address text, never a name or initials, to a mapping service. You can switch this off in Settings and plan with town names only.

Questions

Good to know before you try it.

Is this a medical record or billing system?
No. Alongside Go plans your schedule and routes. It does not store notes or diagnoses, and it does not bill. Use it alongside the system you already document in.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. Open your link on your phone and add it to your Home Screen. Then link it to your computer with private sync, so you can set up once on a bigger screen.
What if I lose my passcode?
You save a recovery code when you set up. We can't reset your passcode for you, because we never have it. Keep your recovery code somewhere safe.
What if I clear my browser's data?
Clearing site data can erase the copy on that device. If you use private sync, link the device again and your information comes back. We also recommend downloading a backup from Settings now and then.
Does it replace electronic visit verification?
No. If your program requires electronic visit verification or timekeeping, keep using that system. Alongside Go is a planning tool.
