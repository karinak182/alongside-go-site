What Alongside Go is

Alongside Go is a planning tool for people who travel to see others on a schedule. It helps you build a weekly schedule, keep track of makeups, prepare text messages, and log mileage. It is not a medical record, a billing system, or a program for electronic visit verification. Please keep using the systems your employer or program requires.
