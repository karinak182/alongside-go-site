Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Alongside Go is made by Alongside OT, a practice based in New York. This page explains, in plain words, what information we handle and what we don't. If anything is unclear, write to hello@alongsidego.com.

The short version

  • Your client list and schedule are saved on your own device and locked with a passcode only you know.
  • We do not have a database of your clients. We can't see your list, and we can't reset your passcode.
  • We keep a small record of who you are as a customer: your name, email, and whether your subscription is active.
  • Your card details go to Stripe, our payment company. We never see or store your card number.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter about the people you visit, including initials, addresses, times, notes about scheduling, and your settings, is saved in your browser on your own device and locked with your passcode. It does not come to us. Please use initials only, and please do not enter diagnoses, clinical notes, or other health information. Alongside Go is a planning tool, not a medical record.

Private sync (optional)

If you turn on sync to use Alongside Go on more than one device, your information is scrambled (encrypted) on your device before it is sent. We store only the scrambled data, an anonymous sync ID, a protected version of your sync key, and time stamps. We can't read it. You can delete your synced copy at any time from Settings, and we will delete it on request.

Maps and driving times

To place visits on a map and work out drive times, the app sends address text (or, if you choose, only a town) to a mapping service, and sends the resulting map points to a driving-time service. We currently use Geoapify, and may use OpenStreetMap's public address search as a backup. Names and initials are never sent. You can switch exact-address lookups off in Settings and plan with town names only. These services have their own privacy policies.

Information we keep about you as a customer

When you start a trial or subscribe, we keep:

  • Your name and email address, and your profession and how you heard about us (if you chose to say).
  • Anything you typed in the referral box, and the referral code we make for you.
  • Your access code, your subscription status, and the last time the app checked that your access is on. The app sends us only the access code when it checks.
  • Identifiers from Stripe that connect your payment record to your account.

We use this to give you access, run your subscription, answer your questions, and track the referral reward. We do not sell your information, and we do not show ads.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and are held by Stripe, not by us. Stripe's privacy policy explains how it uses that information.

Our website

This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Our website is hosted by Cloudflare, which, like any web host, may keep standard technical records such as your IP address. The site's text font loads from Google Fonts, which means Google can see that your browser asked for it.

How long we keep things

We keep your customer record while your account is open. After you cancel, we keep billing and account records for as long as we need them for taxes and accounting, and then delete them. Your data on your own device stays there until you delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we keep about you by writing to hello@alongsidego.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, and we will honor them.

Children

Alongside Go is for professionals. It is not meant to be used by children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and, for big changes, let customers know by email.
